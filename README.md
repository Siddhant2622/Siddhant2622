### 02. Business Entity Resolution & Record Linkage Engine
> **High-cardinality entity resolution system to de-duplicate and link noisy business listings at scale.**
*Developed for the Amazon ML Challenge*

- **Problem**: In raw business directories, identical real-world entities appear with misspelled names, varying address formats, and missing attributes. Direct $O(N^2)$ cross-comparison is computationally prohibitive.
- **Engineering Solution**: Architected a multi-stage entity resolution pipeline. Implemented **blocking mechanisms** in PostgreSQL/Supabase to prune search space by >90%, followed by **multi-feature fuzzy token similarity scoring** and candidate ranking. Built a dedicated **human-in-the-loop review workflow** to inspect borderline matches.
- **Key Technical Highlight**: Calibrated classification threshold targeting the **$F_{0.5}$ metric**, strategically weighting precision higher than recall to minimize false-positive linkages in automated master data management.
- **Tech Stack**: `PostgreSQL`, `Supabase`, `Python`, `Feature Engineering`, `Blocking Algorithms`
- **Links**: [Repository & Code](https://github.com/Siddhant2622) · [Technical Documentation](https://github.com/Siddhant2622)

### 04. AI-Powered Industrial Predictive Maintenance System
> **Hardware-to-cloud telemetry pipeline detecting mechanical degradation before threshold breach.**

- **Problem**: Unscheduled industrial machinery failures cause severe operational downtime; reactive maintenance schedules fail to capture subtle vibration and thermal anomalies.
- **Engineering Solution**: Blended ECE hardware design with machine learning. Instrumented multi-axis vibration and thermal sensors via microcontrollers (Arduino/C++), continuously streaming raw telemetry over serial/REST endpoints into an analytical ML pipeline.
- **Key Technical Highlight**: Engineered statistical time-domain features (RMS, kurtosis, peak-to-peak amplitude) from raw sensor streams, feeding an anomaly detection classifier to estimate Remaining Useful Life (RUL).
- **Tech Stack**: `Embedded C++`, `Arduino`, `Python`, `Machine Learning`, `Sensor Telemetry`, `Signal Processing`
- **Links**: [Repository & Code](https://github.com/Siddhant2622) · [Circuit & Architecture Specs](https://github.com/Siddhant2622)
